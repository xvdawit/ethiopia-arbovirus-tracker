About this project

This dashboard was built as part of an Advanced Virology assignment at Bahir Dar University, College of Medicine and Health Sciences, Department of Medical Microbiology.


Title: The prevailing epidemiology and ecology of arboviruses in Ethiopia

Author: Dawit Baye · ID: BDU-1807458

Instructor: Mr. Awoke Derbie

Methods

Data compiled from systematic searches across PubMed, Google Scholar, and ScienceDirect using terms including "arbovirus Ethiopia", "dengue epidemiology Ethiopia", and "vector-borne disease East Africa". Studies published between 2014–2025 were prioritized. Citation management via Zotero.
